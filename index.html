FluxerCon

What is FluxerCon?

FluxerCon is an event by and for the Fluxer community. Learn more at: https://fluxer.gg/Vx1xRcWR

The Request for Proposal

Hello! The FluxerCon 2027 team (unofficial event) is looking to commission a logo for the event.

FluxerCon is a community-organized, unofficial event to celebrate and discuss Fluxer over a whole day. From interest areas and talks to vendor booths and hopefully a keynote address, FluxerCon aims to be the place where the Fluxer community convenes and energizes. For user safety, FluxerCon 2027 will be an online-only event hosted on a pokemon-game-like proxomity-chat platform (still ironing out the sponsorship) and possibly using Fluxer itself as some of the backing platform.

FluxerCon needs a logo. That's where you come in! We are hosting a competition for choosing a single monochrome vector logo, and choosing multiple "artist's style" logos. The monochrome logo will be the official logo of FluxerCon, used on badges or published materials (if any), etc. The "artist's style" logos that are selected by the judging team will display randomly on each page load of the FluxerCon website, to showcase the community's involvement.

Each submission can apply for either one or BOTH of those logos (monochrome and "artist's style"). One submission per artist.

In a submission, an artist would submit their work and a quote for the price for each item.

The logo judging team will select ONE vector monochrome logo, and MULTIPLE submissions for "artist's style" logos. All winners will be paid for their winning submissions. If an artist wins both the vector monochrome logo submission spot and the "artist's style" spot, both items will be paid. If an artist wins only one of those spots, only the winning submission item will be paid.

Some specifications/parameters about each logo:

  1. The vector monochrome logo is composed of 2 form factors: a square format and a rectangular landscape format (3:1)
  2. The "artist's style" logo is 1 form factor: a rectangular landscape format (3:1)

Any payments will be through PayPal, Xoom, or Wise.

Submit your proposals to: fluxercon@fluxer.host
